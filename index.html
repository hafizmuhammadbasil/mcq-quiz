📋 File Format Guide

TXT format (separate questions with a blank line):
1. What is the capital of France?
A. London
B. Paris
C. Berlin
D. Madrid
Answer: B
Explanation: Paris is the capital of France.

CSV format (header row required):
question,optionA,optionB,optionC,optionD,answer,explanation

JSON format:
[{"question":"2+2=?","options":["3","4","5","6"],"answer":"B","explanation":"Basic math"}]
